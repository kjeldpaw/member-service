# Instructors see the Guardians of their Members, from any Club

A Guardian need not share a Club with the Member they look after, and the
Guardians are how an Instructor reaches a Member without a login. So an
Instructor who sees a Member also sees that Member's Guardians' name, email
and phone, even when a Guardian belongs to none of the Instructor's Clubs.
Like ADR 0002, the exception is narrow: it does not show the Guardian's
other details or give access to the Guardian's own Member record.
